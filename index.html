HOW TO PLAY

Tips for new players: ON

Goal. Reduce the opposing Health Die from 6 to 0. There are no life cards to trash — only the die.
Paragon. Each starter has one god Paragon at 5,000 Power. Only Paragons Awaken, and only at 2 HP or less. Awakened art is a unique inset, never a copy of the main portrait.
Divine. A separate 10-card resource. The first player starts with 2 Divine. Spend Divine to play Characters, Events, Relics, Domains, and Mythics.
Combat. Attack with a ready Character or your Paragon. If the opponent has GUARD, you must strike a Guard. Higher Power wins. Counters add Power when defending.
Keywords. RUSH attacks the turn it enters. CHARGE gains Power when it attacks. GUARD intercepts. FATE triggers when you lose HP.
Starters. ST-01 Host of Ares and ST-02 Thunderforge use different cards. Print them from the companion print-kit repo at 63×88 mm.
